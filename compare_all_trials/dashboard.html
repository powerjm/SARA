Bottom line

Every figure in the companion COMPARATIVE_ANALYSIS.md reproduces exactly. What the numbers describe is a step-up-and-hold, not a rise-and-fall. Trial 1 (0%) had no execution loop — only 2 tools. Trial 2 gave the agent 22 tools and a write-run-debug loop, and capability appeared (37%). Trial 3 held that capability and added the strongest model in the series (Opus, 52.8%) — its lower aggregate is two identified, fixable apparatus artifacts: a premium wall-clock halved to 300 s (100% of Trial-3 premium failures are timeouts) and a block of CGC targets nothing can solve (0/63). The one result that never moves is the CGC wall: 0 successes in 773 records. Part A reads the full matrix; Part B isolates the premier model class.
